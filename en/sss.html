drL IPTV Player Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. Below are 16 direct answers covering playlist setup, playback troubleshooting, privacy and encryption, parental controls, supported Android devices, and Premium subscription management.

What are the most common questions and answers about drL IPTV Player?

1. What is drL IPTV Player and what does it do?

drL IPTV Player is a media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays your own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials. It does not provide or sell any media content.

2. Does the app come with any preloaded channels, movies, or playlists?

No. drL IPTV Player contains no built-in channels, movies, or playlists. You must supply your own legal source.

3. Which devices and platforms are supported?

It supports Android smartphones, Android tablets, and Android TV / Google TV devices. The app is designed exclusively for the Android operating system.

4. Do I need to register an account or sign in?

No. No account creation, email address, or phone number is needed to use the app.

5. Are my M3U links or Xtream passwords sent to your servers?

No. All playlist credentials are encrypted locally on your device via Android Keystore and are only used to request streams directly from the server you configured.

6. How can I add a playlist to Android TV without typing on a remote?

Open 'TV Connection > QR Code' on your Android TV, scan the QR code with your phone camera, and paste your M3U or Xtream details on the local pairing page.

7. Does QR TV Pairing send my data over the internet?

No. QR pairing operates exclusively over your local Wi-Fi network using a temporary port protected by a 6-digit PIN.

8. How do I cast videos to Chromecast?

Tap the Cast icon while connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your Chromecast or Google TV, then select your TV—or enter your TV's local IP address manually.

9. Why is a channel buffering or failing to play?

Buffering or stream errors usually occur when the source IPTV server is overloaded, offline, or blocking connections, or when your local network is unstable.

10. Why does the app request Camera and Local Network permissions?

Camera permission is used only when scanning a TV QR code (no images are stored). Local network access is used only for Chromecast discovery and local QR TV pairing.

11. Does the free version contain advertisements?

Yes. The free version displays Google AdMob ads. Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are presented with a Google UMP consent dialog.

12. What is included in the optional Premium subscription?

Premium removes advertisements and unlocks Catch-up (on supported providers) and Multi-Screen playback.

13. How do I cancel a Premium subscription?

All billing is handled by Google Play. You can cancel anytime under Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

14. How does Parental Control work?

You can set a 4-digit PIN in Settings to lock adult content categories and restrict access across family profiles.

15. What is Lite Mode and when should I enable it?

Lite Mode disables heavy visual effects so that older phones and low-powered Android TV boxes run smoothly. You can enable it in Settings > General.

16. How do I delete all my data from the app?

You can delete your saved playlists and watch history directly inside Settings, or uninstall the app to erase all locally encrypted data from your device.
