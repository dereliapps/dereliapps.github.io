How to Pair Your Phone with Android TV via QR Code

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. QR TV Pairing establishes a temporary, 6-digit PIN-protected local Wi-Fi link between your phone and Android TV so you can send playlists without typing on a TV remote.

Get it on Google Play

How do you pair your phone and Android TV step by step?

  1. Connect both your Android TV and your smartphone to the same local Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open drL IPTV Player on your Android TV and navigate to 'TV Connection > QR Code' (or select the QR pairing card on the home screen).
  3. Note the QR code and the 6-digit security PIN displayed on your TV screen.
  4. Scan the QR code using your phone's camera or the built-in QR scanner inside the mobile drL IPTV Player app.
  5. On the local web page that opens on your phone, verify the 6-digit PIN, paste your M3U URL or Xtream credentials, and tap 'Send to TV'.
  6. Approve the confirmation prompt on your Android TV screen to save the playlist.

Is QR TV Pairing secure and does data leave my local network?

QR pairing operates strictly over your local area network (LAN); no playlist data is routed through external servers or the public internet. The temporary local port is active only while the QR screen is open on the TV, requires a 6-digit PIN, locks for 1 minute after 5 failed attempts, and requires explicit confirmation on the TV screen. Camera permission is used solely to scan the QR code.

What are common questions about QR TV Pairing?

Do I need the app installed on my phone to send a playlist to my TV?

No. Scanning the TV's QR code with your standard phone camera opens a local browser page hosted by your TV, allowing you to paste and send your playlist from any phone browser.

What if the QR page does not open on my phone?

Ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, disable any active VPN, and verify that router 'AP Isolation' (Client Isolation) is turned off.

Can I send an already configured playlist from the mobile app to my TV?

Yes. Using the in-app QR scanner on your Android phone, you can push an existing playlist or currently playing stream directly to your TV.
