How to Cast Streams to Chromecast and Troubleshoot Connections

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. You can automatically discover Chromecast and Google Cast-compatible TVs on your Wi-Fi network and stream Live TV or VOD content to the big screen with a single tap.

Get it on Google Play

How do you cast a stream to Chromecast step by step?

  1. Connect your Android phone or tablet to the same Wi-Fi network as your Chromecast or Google TV device.
  2. Open drL IPTV Player, start playing a channel or video (or tap the Cast icon in the top navigation bar).
  3. Under the 'Chromecast' tab, select your television (e.g., Smart TV) from the list of discovered devices.
  4. If your TV does not appear automatically, enter the TV's local IP address (found in TV Settings > Network) into the manual connection box and tap 'Connect'.
  5. Once connected, playback begins on your TV while your phone acts as a remote for play, pause, and volume.

How do you troubleshoot Chromecast discovery or playback issues?

Device not appearing in list

Check that your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, turn off VPN apps, and tap 'Rescan'. You can also connect directly using your TV's local IP address.

Stream loads on phone but fails on TV

Many IPTV servers omit CORS headers required by Cast receivers. drL IPTV Player uses a built-in local proxy server to rewrite headers and HLS segments for Cast compatibility.

Using Screen Mirroring as an alternative

If your TV does not support Google Cast, switch to the 'Screen Mirror' tab inside the TV Connection menu to open Android's system screen mirroring utility.

What are common questions about Chromecast support?

Why does Chromecast require local network permission?

Local network and Wi-Fi multicast permissions are required solely to discover Cast devices on your home Wi-Fi and deliver the media stream to your TV.

Can I connect to my Chromecast TV manually by IP address?

Yes. If multicast discovery is blocked by your router, you can type your TV's local IP address into the manual connection field.

Does disconnecting stop playback on the TV?

Yes. Disconnecting from the Cast session inside drL IPTV Player cleanly closes the receiver session on your TV.
