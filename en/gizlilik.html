Privacy Policy — drL IPTV Player

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. Last updated: October 20, 2026. This policy explains what data is processed and how it is protected when you use drL IPTV Player, developed by drL Yazılım.

1. Is an account required to use the application?

No membership, email address, or phone number is required to use the application. As the developer, we do not maintain any user accounts that identify you.

2. What data is stored on your device and is it sent to us?

Playlist credentials (M3U URLs, Xtream server URL, username, and password) are stored exclusively on your device in encrypted storage protected by Android Keystore and are sent only to the IPTV server you specify to request streams; they are never sent to us or third parties. Watch history, favorites, profiles, parental control PIN (hashed), and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data, and you can also delete playlists and history within the app.

3. How do advertisements (Google AdMob) process data?

The free version of the application displays Google AdMob advertisements. AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and application information, approximate location, and ad interactions in accordance with Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); you can change your preference in Settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. A Premium subscription removes ads.

4. How are purchases and billing handled?

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing. We do not see or store your payment information; we only receive confirmation from Google Play regarding whether your subscription is active.

5. Why is camera permission used?

The camera is opened only to scan the QR code on your TV screen when you actively use that feature. Images are never recorded or transmitted anywhere.

6. Why is local network access used?

Local network access is used to discover Chromecast / Google TV devices on the same Wi-Fi network and cast streams to them. When you open the phone pairing (QR) feature, your device opens a temporary local port protected by a 6-digit PIN; this connection works strictly between your devices on the local network and closes when you leave the screen. Data does not leave your local network.

7. Which system permissions does the app request?

Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state / multicast (device discovery), camera (QR scanning), notifications, and—if you use the download feature—storage. Permissions are used solely for their corresponding feature.

8. How is children's privacy addressed?

The application is not directed to children under the age of 13. Parental controls (PIN and adult content filter) are provided for families.

9. Are crash reports or analytics collected?

We currently do not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting services. If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated.

10. What are your rights and how can you delete your data?

Because your playlist and usage data are not stored on our servers, data deletion is completed by clearing your data inside the app (Settings > Delete Playlist/History) or uninstalling the application. For inquiries regarding your rights under GDPR or KVKK, contact us at dereliticaret61@gmail.com.

11. Who is responsible for media content?

The user is responsible for ensuring that the sources they add are legal and properly licensed. The application is not a content provider.

12. How are policy changes communicated and how can you contact us?

This policy may be updated from time to time, and the current version is always published on this page. Contact: drL Yazılım — dereliticaret61@gmail.com
