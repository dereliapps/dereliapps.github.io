What should you include when requesting technical support?

To help us diagnose technical issues quickly, please mention your device model (phone, tablet, or Android TV model), Android OS version, and the steps that led to the issue. For your security, NEVER send your private M3U URLs or Xtream Codes usernames and passwords in support emails. Please note that we do not sell or provide IPTV subscriptions or channel lists.

What are common questions before contacting support?

Can I contact you to buy an IPTV subscription or channel list?

No. drL IPTV Player is strictly a software media player; we do not sell, host, or recommend any IPTV subscriptions or channels.

Do I need to email support to cancel my Premium subscription?

No. You can cancel your subscription immediately at any time through the Google Play Store Subscriptions menu.

Where do I send GDPR or KVKK privacy inquiries?

Send all privacy and data rights inquiries to dereliticaret61@gmail.com.
