What is Xtream Codes? How It Works and How It Differs from M3U

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. Xtream Codes API is a standardized communication protocol that lets a media player retrieve structured Live TV, movie, series, and EPG data using a server URL, username, and password.

How does the Xtream Codes protocol work?

Instead of pasting a single static playlist file URL, the user enters three credentials provided by their authorized service: Server URL, Username, and Password. The media player queries standardized JSON endpoints on that server to load live categories, video-on-demand (VOD) catalogs, multi-season series hierarchies, and Electronic Program Guide (EPG) data.

How does Xtream Codes differ from an M3U playlist?

Simpler Credential Entry

Entering a short server URL, username, and password is less error-prone than typing a long M3U query string.

Structured Series & Seasons

TV series are organized by show, season, and episode rather than appearing as thousands of flat entries.

Integrated EPG & Catch-up

Program guide schedules and Catch-up metadata (when supported by the provider) are retrieved automatically without adding a separate XMLTV URL.

How does drL IPTV Player handle Xtream Codes security and legal compliance?

Your Xtream Codes server URL, username, and password are encrypted locally on your device using Android Keystore and are transmitted only to the server you specify. drL IPTV Player does not sell or provide Xtream Codes accounts; users are responsible for verifying the legality of their sources.

What are common questions about Xtream Codes?

Where do I get Xtream Codes login details?

You obtain them from your own legal IPTV service provider. drL IPTV Player does not sell subscriptions or provide credentials.

Does drL IPTV Player handle large Xtream catalogs smoothly?

Yes. JSON decoding is performed in a background isolate with a 60-second timeout, preventing UI stutters even on large catalogs.

Can I enter Xtream credentials on Android TV using my phone?

Yes. Open QR TV Pairing on your Android TV, scan the QR code with your phone, and paste your Server URL, Username, and Password from your phone browser.
