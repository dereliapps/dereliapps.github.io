drL IPTV Player: Android & Android TV Media Player for Your M3U and Xtream Playlists

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. It lets you pair your phone with your TV via QR code in seconds, cast streams to Chromecast devices, and keep your playlist credentials encrypted locally on your device.

Get it on Google Play Setup Guide

What features does drL IPTV Player offer?

M3U & Xtream Codes Support

Add your own legal subscription in seconds using standard M3U/M3U8 URLs or Xtream Codes credentials (server URL, username, password).

QR Code TV Pairing

Scan the QR code displayed on your Android TV with your phone to send playlists or streams without typing long URLs on a remote control.

Chromecast & Google TV

Automatically discover Chromecast and Google Cast-enabled TVs on the same Wi-Fi network and cast live streams or videos with one tap.

Live TV, Movies & Series

Organized categories, fast search, favorites list, selectable audio and subtitle tracks, and resume playback from where you left off.

Parental Control & Multi-Profile

Create separate user profiles for family members and protect adult content categories with a 4-digit PIN.

EPG, PiP & Sleep Timer

Electronic Program Guide (EPG), Picture-in-Picture (PiP) multitasking, screen lock, and customizable sleep timer.

Android TV D-pad & Lite Mode

Full D-pad remote navigation for Android TV and a Lite Mode that disables heavy visual effects on low-end devices.

Local Encrypted Privacy

No account registration required; your playlist credentials are encrypted locally via Android Keystore and never sent to our servers.

How are your data and privacy protected?

You do not need to create an account or provide an email address to use drL IPTV Player. Your M3U URLs and Xtream Codes credentials are stored exclusively on your device using Android Keystore encryption. The free version displays Google AdMob ads (with user consent requested in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland); an optional Premium subscription provides ad-free viewing, Catch-up (on supported providers), and Multi-Screen support.

What are the most frequently asked questions about drL IPTV Player?

Does drL IPTV Player include built-in channels or movies?

No. drL IPTV Player is strictly a media player and does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series. You must add your own legal M3U or Xtream Codes source.

Which devices are supported?

It runs on Android phones, Android tablets, and Android TV / Google TV devices. Only Android operating system devices are supported.

Do I need to create an account to use the app?

No. No registration, email, or login account is required. You simply install the app and add your own playlist.

How do I avoid typing long M3U URLs on my Android TV remote?

Use the built-in QR TV Pairing feature: open the QR screen on your TV, scan it with your phone camera, and send your playlist directly over your local Wi-Fi network.

How can I cancel my Premium subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through Google Play and can be canceled anytime from the Subscriptions section of the Google Play Store app.
