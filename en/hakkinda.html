About drL IPTV Player

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. Developed by drL Yazılım, the application focuses on responsive playback, local data privacy, and seamless interaction between Android phones and Android TV.

Why was drL IPTV Player built?

drL IPTV Player was engineered to give Android phone, tablet, and Android TV users a clean, reliable way to play their own authorized M3U and Xtream Codes playlists. By combining background-isolate playlist parsing, local QR TV pairing, and a custom CASTV2 Chromecast bridge, it eliminates common frustrations like typing long URLs with a TV remote.

Is drL IPTV Player free to download?

Yes. The app is free to download on Google Play and includes M3U/Xtream playback, QR TV pairing, and Chromecast support. The free version is supported by Google AdMob ads, while optional Premium unlocks ad-free viewing, Catch-up, and Multi-Screen.
