How to Set Up drL IPTV Player: Step-by-Step M3U and Xtream Guide

drL IPTV Player is a modern media player for Android phones, tablets, and Android TV that plays the user's own M3U playlists or Xtream Codes credentials; the app does not provide or sell any channels, movies, or series content. To get started, install the app from Google Play and enter the M3U playlist URL or Xtream Codes credentials provided by your legal IPTV service.

Get it on Google Play QR TV Pairing

How do you add an M3U playlist step by step?

  1. Download and open drL IPTV Player from the Google Play Store on your Android phone, tablet, or Android TV.
  2. Complete the brief onboarding screen and select 'M3U URL' on the 'Add Playlist' screen.
  3. Enter a recognizable name for your playlist (e.g., 'Living Room TV') and paste the http:// or https:// M3U / M3U8 URL from your authorized provider.
  4. Tap 'Save & Load'; drL IPTV Player parses your playlist in a background isolate and organizes Live TV, movies, and series categories.

How do you add Xtream Codes credentials step by step?

  1. Open the 'Add Playlist' menu inside the app and select the 'Xtream Codes API' tab.
  2. Enter a profile name along with the Server URL, Username, and Password supplied by your legal provider.
  3. If you are setting up Android TV, you can open 'QR TV Pairing' instead of typing with the remote and send these details from your phone.
  4. Confirm the connection; the app automatically loads live channels, VOD movies, series episodes, and EPG program schedules.

What should you do if a playlist fails to load?

Verify that your internet connection is active and check for accidental spaces or typos in the server URL. Very large playlists may take several seconds on initial load depending on your provider's server speed; the app uses a 60-second timeout and background parsing to prevent UI freezes.

What are common questions about setting up playlists?

Can I add multiple M3U or Xtream playlists?

Yes. drL IPTV Player supports multiple playlists and user profiles, allowing you to switch between sources effortlessly.

Are my server URLs and passwords stored securely?

Yes. Credentials are encrypted locally on your device using Android Keystore and are never uploaded to developer servers.

Will large playlists freeze the app interface?

No. Playlist parsing runs in a background isolate so navigation remains responsive even with large channel lists.
